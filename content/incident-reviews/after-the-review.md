# After the review

## Agree actions

Agree a small number of actions, each with an owner and a date. Prefer
changes to systems and processes over reminders to be more careful.

## Share what you learnt

Publish the write-up where other teams can read it. Your contributing
factors may be present in their systems too.

## Follow up

Check progress on the actions at your next team retrospective, and close
the review only when they are done.
