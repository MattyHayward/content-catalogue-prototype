# Running the review

## Build the timeline together

Walk through the incident in order. At each point, ask what people knew,
what they expected, and why their next action made sense to them.

## Look for contributing factors

Incidents rarely have one root cause. Record each condition that made the
incident possible or worse: missing alerts, unclear runbooks, risky
defaults, time pressure.

## Keep it blameless

If the conversation turns to who did something, turn it back to why it made
sense at the time and what would have helped.
