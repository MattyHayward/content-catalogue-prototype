# Before the review

## Choose a facilitator

Ask someone who was not involved in the incident to facilitate. Their job is
to keep the review blameless and moving, not to judge.

## Gather the facts

Collect the timeline from alerts, logs, chat channels and tickets before the
meeting, so the review spends its time on understanding rather than
reconstruction.

## Invite the right people

Invite everyone who responded, anyone whose decisions shaped the incident,
and the people who own the systems involved.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Tell everyone in the invitation that the review is blameless, and what that
> means.
