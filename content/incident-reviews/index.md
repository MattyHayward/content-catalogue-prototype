# Running a blameless incident review

Hold a review after any incident that affected users, or that could have.
A review is blameless when it looks for the conditions that made an action
seem sensible at the time, rather than for who made a mistake. People share
what really happened only when they are not afraid of being blamed for it.

The method has three steps:

1. [Before the review](before-the-review.md): gather the facts and the people.
2. [Running the review](running-the-review.md): build the timeline and find the causes.
3. [After the review](after-the-review.md): agree actions, share what you learnt, follow up.

> [!NOTE]
> Hold the review within 5 working days of the incident, while people still
> remember the details.
