# Callouts

Callouts use GitHub's alert syntax, so they look right on GitHub as well as on
the site.

## Notes and tips

These become GOV.UK inset text.

> [!NOTE]
> Something the reader might otherwise miss.

> [!TIP]
> A shortcut or a better way of doing something.

A plain quote becomes inset text too:

> Use a quote for something said or written elsewhere.

## Warnings

These become GOV.UK warning text. Keep them for things that matter: if every
other paragraph is a warning, none of them is.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Something the reader must do.

> [!WARNING]
> Something that could go wrong if they do not.

> [!CAUTION]
> Something with serious consequences.
